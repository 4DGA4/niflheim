## Email Best Practices

### Subject Line Tips
- Keep it under 60 characters
- Mention specific value proposition
- Personalize when possible
- Avoid spam trigger words

### Content Tips
- Keep emails under 300 words when possible
- Use short paragraphs and bullet points
- Include clear call-to-action
- Personalize for recipient's context
- Proofread carefully

### Timing
- **Best**: Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM recipient's timezone
- **Avoid**: Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, weekends
- **Follow-up**: 1 week and 2 weeks after initial email

### Tracking
- Use email tracking if appropriate (with consent)
- Monitor open rates and click-through
- A/B test subject lines
- Adjust messaging based on response rates

### Legal Compliance
- Include unsubscribe option if sending bulk emails
- Comply with CAN-SPAM Act (US)
- Follow GDPR requirements (EU)
- Respect recipient preferences
